## Definition

The **Network layer** provides logical addressing and routing so packets can travel across multiple interconnected networks.

## Responsibilities

- Logical addressing (IP addresses)
- Routing and forwarding decisions
- Best-effort packet delivery between hosts
- Packet fragmentation behavior (version-dependent and path-dependent)

## Core Protocol

- [[Internet Protocol (IP)|IP (Internet Protocol)]] is the primary network-layer protocol of the Internet.
- [[Internet Control Message Protocol (ICMP)|ICMP]] reports errors and supports diagnostics alongside IP.

## Key Idea

Routers operate primarily at this layer, selecting next hops to move packets toward their destination.
