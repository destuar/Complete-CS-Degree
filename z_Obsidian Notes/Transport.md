## Definition

The **Transport layer** provides end-to-end communication between application processes on different hosts.

## Responsibilities

- Process-to-process delivery using port numbers
- Multiplexing and demultiplexing of application streams
- Reliability and ordering (with TCP)
- Flow/congestion-related mechanisms (protocol-dependent)

## Main Internet Protocols

- [[Transmission Control Protocol (TCP)|TCP]]: connection-oriented, reliable, ordered byte stream
- [[User Datagram Protocol (UDP)|UDP]]: connectionless, minimal overhead, best-effort datagrams

## Why It Matters

Application behavior (latency, reliability, throughput) is strongly affected by transport protocol choice.
